# Is There a Tape? A Bayesian Look at the Trump–Epstein Video Question

*Prepared 17 November 2026. All probabilities are subjective estimates built from public reporting. Donald Trump denies any wrongdoing connected to Jeffrey Epstein and has not been charged with any crime related to him.*

## The question

**H:** A video recording exists today, in someone's possession, that shows Donald Trump in sexual activity with a person under 18.

**Bottom line:** about **0.4%**, with a plausible range of roughly **0.03% to 5%**. No public evidence shows such a video exists. The estimate is above zero because of how Epstein operated and how long the two men were friends, not because of any direct evidence of a tape.

## Method

H needs three things to be true at once, so the prior is a product:

| Step | Claim | Estimate | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Trump had sexual contact with a minor in a setting where recording was possible | 8% | 3–20% |
| B | It was recorded on video, given A | 20% | 10–35% |
| C | The recording still exists and someone holds it, given A and B | 50% | 30–60% |
| | **Prior for H** (A × B × C) | **0.8%** | 0.09–4.2% |

Each piece of evidence then gets a likelihood ratio (LR): how much more likely we would be to see it if H were true than if H were false. LR above 1 raises the estimate, below 1 lowers it, and 1 changes nothing. Odds are multiplied by each LR in turn.

### Why those priors

- **A (8%).** Trump and Epstein were friends for roughly 15 years. Epstein's own emails claim Trump "knew about the girls" and "spent hours at my house" with a victim ([AP via WABE](https://www.wabe.org/epstein-email-says-trump-knew-about-the-girls-as-white-house-calls-its-release-a-democratic-smear/)). A 2016 civil suit by "Katie Johnson" alleged rape at age 13 in 1994; it was withdrawn before any court tested it and reporters could not verify her identity ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_sexual_misconduct_allegations)). Against that: Virginia Giuffre said under oath she did not believe Trump knew of Epstein's misconduct ([AP via WABE](https://www.wabe.org/epstein-email-says-trump-knew-about-the-girls-as-white-house-calls-its-release-a-democratic-smear/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/30/virginia-giuffre-trump-epstein-maxwell)), and a 2019 prosecution memo said no other victim described being directed to have sex with other men ([AP via WTOP](https://wtop.com/national/2026/02/fbi-concluded-jeffrey-epstein-wasnt-running-a-sex-trafficking-ring-for-powerful-men-files-show/)).
- **B (20%).** Victims describe hidden cameras and monitoring rooms in Epstein's homes ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/jeffrey-epstein-accuser-maria-farmer-says-his-house-under-constant-tv-surveillance)). But the conduct, if it happened, need not have been at an Epstein property, and investigators say the seized media showed no abuse at all (see E2).
- **C (50%).** Epstein hid and moved evidence for years ([ABC News](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/epstein-hid-trove-evidence-investigators-decade-documents-suggest-130494832)); hard drives and CDs left his safe for days in 2019 before his lawyer handed them over ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.in/international/news/fbi-agents-used-a-saw-to-open-a-safe-in-jeffrey-epsteins-manhattan-mansion-that-held-hard-drives-and-diamonds/articleshow/88134237.cms)). Material could have been destroyed or could sit with a third party.

## The evidence, one update at a time

Starting point: 0.8% (odds 0.0081).

| # | Evidence | LR | Estimate after |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1+E2 | Biden-era DOJ neither released nor prosecuted; investigators say seized media shows no abuse | 0.45 | 0.36% |
| E3 | The released files themselves | 1.05 | 0.38% |
| E4 | Ted Lieu's statement | 1.0 | 0.38% |
| E5 | Iran "threat" | 1.0 | 0.38% |
| E6 | Ukrainian ex-general and Christopher Steele on Russian kompromat | 1.1 | 0.42% |
| E7 | The Trump administration's handling of the files | 1.5 | 0.63% |
| E8 | Epstein's private emails | 1.0 | 0.63% |
| E9 | A decade of intense searching with nothing surfacing | 0.6 | **0.38%** |

### E1 + E2. Biden didn't release or prosecute; the FBI says the media shows no abuse (LR 0.45)

From 2021 to 2025 a Democratic Justice Department held everything seized from Epstein while Trump was the opposing party's leading figure. If a video of him abusing a minor were in that evidence, non-prosecution and four years of silence would be very hard to explain. The ordinary explanation for non-release is that the Maxwell case was still on appeal ([Julie K. Brown via NewsNation](https://digital-release.newsnationnow.com/?p=3632322)); Trump has instead suggested the prior administration tampered with the files, without evidence ([ABC News](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/trump-facing-maga-uproar-epstein-files-shift-blame-123833646)).

Separately, a 2025 prosecutor memo says videos and photos seized from Epstein's homes in New York, Florida and the Virgin Islands did not depict victims being abused or implicate anyone else, and the FBI concluded Epstein was not running a trafficking ring for powerful men ([AP via WTOP](https://wtop.com/national/2026/02/fbi-concluded-jeffrey-epstein-wasnt-running-a-sex-trafficking-ring-for-powerful-men-files-show/)). The July 2025 DOJ/FBI memo found no client list and no evidence to open cases against third parties ([Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/epstein-client-list-doesnt-exist-justice-department-says-walking-back-theory-bondi-had-promoted/); [memo PDF](https://edgarcountywatchdogs.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DOJ-FBI-Memorandum-on-Epstien-files.pdf)).

These two facts nearly rule out "the US government holds the tape." They say little about a tape held by someone else, which is why the combined LR is 0.45 and not far lower. They are also correlated, so they are counted once.

### E3. The actual files (LR 1.05)

The January 2026 release ran to more than 3 million pages, over 2,000 videos and 180,000 images ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/01/30/nx-s1-5693904/epstein-files-doj-trump); [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/epstein-files-released-doj-2026/)). Trump is mentioned thousands of times ([ABC Australia](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-02-03/donald-trump-mentioned-in-epstein-files-thousands-of-times/106297878); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/31/politics/new-documents-trump-epstein)). The files include allegations against him, many from an FBI public tip line, some anonymous or second-hand. Deputy AG Todd Blanche said the department found no credible information meriting further investigation and that some tips could not be investigated ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/5717221-trump-epstein-tips-doj-investigation/)). One widely shared tip did not fit the known timeline ([Snopes via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/fact-check-unverified-fbi-tip-030000451.html)).

Two effects roughly cancel. The existence of allegations nudges step A up (about ×1.3). But no video of Trump appeared among thousands released, and none has leaked from the many officials, lawyers and congressional staff who have seen unredacted material (about ×0.8). Caveats: the release was late, is under Inspector General review, and some files vanished from the DOJ site ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/23/us-department-of-justice-watchdog-to-probe-release-of-epstein-files); [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/jeffrey-epstein-file-468-least-16-files-disappear-doj-library-including-photo-showing-donald-trump/18304229/)).

### E4. Ted Lieu "saying there is a video" (LR 1.0)

He did not say that. On 3 February 2026 Lieu referred to "highly disturbing allegations" in the files that Trump raped and threatened children. His office told Snopes he meant allegations, not video. The "video" quote comes from memes and AI-generated clips ([Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/ted-lieu-epstein-files-trump/); [Lead Stories via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/fact-check-rep-ted-lieu-021735545.html); [Snopes on the "senator saw a video" rumor](https://www.snopes.com/news/2026/03/11/trump-child-rape-video/)). The underlying allegations are already counted in E3, so this adds nothing.

### E5. Iran threatening to release (LR 1.0)

I found no reporting of an Iranian threat to release Epstein material. What exists is a mocking video from Iran's embassy in Indonesia showing a UFO with a "RELEASE EPSTEIN FILES" banner ([OneIndia](https://www.oneindia.com/videos/iran-mocks-trump-with-release-epstein-files-ufo-meme-banner-targets-trump-and-epstein-secrets-4305702.html)). Trolling is equally likely whether or not a tape exists. If you have a specific source for a real threat, this entry should be revisited.

### E6. Ukraine saying Russia has kompromat (LR 1.1)

This is not an official Ukrainian finding. It is a claim by Grigory Omelchenko, a retired SBU lieutenant general, in a TV interview ([Dagens](https://www.dagens.com/news/putin-holds-devastating-epstein-linked-dirt-on-trump-claims-ukrainian-general)). Christopher Steele has said it is "quite likely" Epstein had compromising material on Trump ([LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/christopher-steele-epstein-trump-russia-5HjdRrM_2/)). Neither offers evidence, both have reasons to say it regardless, and "kompromat" could mean financial material. Small upward nudge only.

### E7. How the Trump administration handled the files (LR 1.5)

Promising release, then the July 2025 reversal, a missed statutory deadline, heavy redactions and disputes over compliance ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/justice-department/doj-releases-new-trove-long-awaited-epstein-files-rcna256714); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/alisondurkee/2026/07/02/could-more-epstein-files-be-released-today-doj-faces-court-ordered-deadline-to-unredact-names/)). This is more likely if there is something seriously damaging to hide. But it is also what you would expect from ordinary embarrassment about a long friendship with Epstein, so the ratio is modest. This is the strongest upward item.

### E8. Epstein's own emails (LR 1.0)

The emails raise the chance that Trump knew more than he says (×1.3 on step A). But in them Epstein talks with Michael Wolff about how to use Trump's denials for leverage ([AP via WABE](https://www.wabe.org/epstein-email-says-trump-knew-about-the-girls-as-white-house-calls-its-release-a-democratic-smear/)). A man holding a tape would not need that kind of leverage (×0.75 on the video). Net: no change.

### E9. Nothing has surfaced (LR 0.6)

Since 2015 Trump has been the most heavily investigated person on earth: opposition research, foreign intelligence services, a special counsel, journalists, and now a statutory document release. A tape held by a private person would be worth a fortune and would probably have appeared. A tape held by a hostile state could stay hidden, which is why this is 0.6 and not 0.2.

## Sensitivity

| Scenario | Prior | Combined LR | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skeptical | 0.09% | 0.3 | 0.03% |
| Central | 0.8% | 0.47 | 0.4% |
| Generous to H | 4.2% | 1.2 | 5% |

The answer is driven mostly by the prior, which is the least certain part. Reasonable people could put step A anywhere from 3% to 20%.

## What would change this

- **Up sharply:** an on-record official or named witness saying they have seen such a video; a forensic authentication; the Inspector General finding that video evidence was withheld.
- **Down:** unredacted evidence logs confirming no camera footage from the relevant years; credible testimony from Epstein staff that recordings of guests were never made.

## Limits

- The likelihood ratios are judgments, not measurements.
- Several items are not independent, and I have tried to count overlapping ones once.
- I could not confirm two of the premises as stated (the Lieu video claim and an Iranian threat); both appear to be distorted versions of real events.
- This estimates whether a *video* exists. It is not an estimate of whether the underlying allegations are true, beyond the rough input in step A.
